# Anything launcher

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Minecraft: Java Edition players on Windows who want mods without modding. They open the
launcher to do one of three things: get the game running (Microsoft sign-in, install 26.3 on
Horizon, fetch game files), join a server, or type a mod and have it land in that server.
Every screen has one obvious next action; the player never has to think.

## Brand personality
Calm, exact, quietly confident. The game is the loud part; the launcher is the quiet frame.

## References
- Layout: Modrinth App (left icon rail, instance header with a big Play, server rows, settings).
- Look: Orion's neutral ElevenLabs style (Bedrock `Approved/Preferences`, `Me/Likes.md`):
  off-white or near-black, black buttons, greys, hairlines, light headlines, mono for data,
  soft corners. Light and dark, following the system, with a toggle.
- Brand tokens: the product site (`apps/web/app/globals.css`), including its one accent, the
  cyan that marks anything the AI made.

## Anti-references
Default AI looks, gradients, glass, gold, rainbow palettes, Minecraft/Mojang art or logos
(original art only), walls of text, uppercase eyebrows over every section, waiting screens that
hide the work, statuses that are guessed rather than true.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast in both themes, full keyboard paths with visible focus, reduced motion respected.

Sources: the task brief from Orion's boss session (2026-11-09) and Bedrock notes; no separate
interview was run.
